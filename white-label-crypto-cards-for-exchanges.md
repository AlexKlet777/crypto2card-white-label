## How an exchange can use the platform

1. Introduce your existing audience to your branded Web or Telegram application.
2. Eligible customers complete the onboarding and verification required by the selected programme.
3. Customers fund the supported service with USDT and use available cards for online or in-store purchases.
4. Your team manages the customer relationship and reviews activity through the agreed Back Office and analytics tools.

Supported countries, funding flows, card networks, fees and limits are confirmed for your project. Apple Pay and Google Pay depend on the selected programme.

## What your business controls

Your branded interface, distribution and customer acquisition remain central to the offer. The agreed setup can include localization, a landing page, referral tools, CRM and transaction statistics. Technology integrations connect the application to the relevant service providers.

For an exchange with an existing audience, the commercial question is whether repeat usage supports a sustainable service. Request a model based on your expected active customers, funding volumes, programme costs and support needs. Revenue and payback depend on actual usage and negotiated terms.

## White Label or API for an exchange?

A complete White Label fits a business that wants a branded customer application with operational tools. If you already have an application and developers, an [API integration](https://alexklet777.github.io/crypto2card-white-label/crypto-card-api/) may better fit your product.

## Questions to settle before launch

- Which countries will your customers reside in?
- How many customers can you reach through your existing distribution?
- What monthly funding volume do you realistically expect?
- Who will handle customer support and escalation?
- Do you need both Web and Telegram, or integration into an existing product?

These answers guide programme availability, integration scope and the commercial proposal. White Label software does not transfer an issuer's or service provider's regulatory status to your business.
