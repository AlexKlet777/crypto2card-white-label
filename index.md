## Frequently asked questions

### Can users fund cards with USDT?

Yes. The platform supports USDT funding and crypto processing.

### Does the solution include Visa or Mastercard cards?

Visa / Mastercard card programmes are available subject to country, KYC requirements and the relevant service provider's availability.

### Is this only a Telegram solution?

No. CRYPTO2Card supports branded Web and Telegram applications. Custom integrations and API work are available on request.

### What does the White Label partner receive?

The partner receives a branded customer interface plus the agreed operational tools, which can include Back Office, CRM, analytics, transaction statistics, referral tools, localization and custom integrations.

### Which countries are supported?

Geographic coverage depends on the specific card programme, provider, KYC/KYB requirements and service availability. Target countries are confirmed during project scoping before final commercial terms.

### How much does a White Label launch cost?

Pricing is provided as a **custom quote**. Commercial terms depend on the selected scope, customization, optional services, integrations and specific provider costs.

### Is a White Label partner using CryptoStoryBank's licences?

No. A White Label partner does not obtain the regulatory status or licences of CSB Technologies Inc. or any Service Provider solely by using the platform.
